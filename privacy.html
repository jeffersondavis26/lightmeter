Talking Light Meter Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

Talking Light Meter does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Camera

The app uses your iPhone's camera only to measure light. Camera images are processed on your phone, in memory, and are never saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.

Settings

Your settings, such as ISO, aperture and film choice, are saved only on your phone.

Tips

Optional tips are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details.

No tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking of any kind.

Contact

Questions? Visit the support page.
