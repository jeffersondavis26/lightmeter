Talking Light Meter Support

Talking Light Meter is a light meter for film photographers that speaks the shutter speed out loud. It is designed for blind and low-vision photographers and works fully with VoiceOver.

Quick start

  1. Set ISO and aperture. With VoiceOver, swipe up or down on each control to change it.
  2. Choose Average or Spot metering.
  3. Point the back camera at your subject and double-tap Measure. The app vibrates and speaks the shutter speed.

Tips

  • If readings are a steady amount off from another meter, use the Calibration control.
  • Choose your film under Film reciprocity to get corrected times for long exposures.
  • Turn on Continuous readout to hear the shutter speed whenever the light changes.
